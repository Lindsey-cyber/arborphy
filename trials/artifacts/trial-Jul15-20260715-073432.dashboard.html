correct_rate correct_count feature_count outcome == 'CORRECT' Best single end-to-end success rate when INCONCLUSIVE should count as not solved.
wrong_rate wrong_count feature_count outcome == 'WRONG' Risk signal: how often the model gives a concrete wrong value.
